The Maker

The chef behind the blends

JP studied culinary arts at Le Cordon Bleu Peru and has cooked in Lima, Berlin, and Miami. His background includes a culinary-advisor role at one of The World’s 50 Best Restaurants, where he helped guests understand the dishes and their ingredients.

His experience spans Peruvian and Mexican cooking, sushi, and Eastern Mediterranean food, with flavors ranging from fresh herbs and citrus to smoke and savory depth. He draws on that experience to make spice blends for home cooks.
